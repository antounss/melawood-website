Our Story

Raised in sawdust.
shaped by family.

MelaWood didn't start as a business. It started in a father's carpentry workshop, with a curious kid who wouldn't leave the workbench.

"I grew up surrounded by the smell of fresh-cut wood and the sound of my father's tools. Before I knew what a career was, I knew how to sand, measure twice, and respect the grain."

Our founder learned the craft the traditional way: by watching, helping, and slowly being trusted with more. Years in his father's carpentry taught him patience, precision, and that a piece is only finished when it's right.

MelaWood is what came next — a new generation bringing modern design into a family trade. Clean lines, honest materials, and the occasional unexpected detail that makes you look twice.
